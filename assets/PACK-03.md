## 実際に使用した生成プロンプト / 制作指示

今回のPNG/SVGは外部作品の切り抜きではなく、再現可能なプロシージャル描画で新規生成しています。以下は各素材の制作時に使用した具体的な指示です。

### 四芒星テックチェーン
Transparent Y2K flyer ornament: five consecutive elongated four-point stars connected by ultra-thin technical guide lines and micro dots, crisp white geometry with tiny acid-lime centers, lots of transparent padding, no text, no logo, no checkerboard background.

### ワイヤー・グローブ02
Transparent retro-futurist wireframe globe: thin white latitude/longitude curves, clean spherical construction, one acid-lime tilted orbital ellipse, precise technical-drawing feel, centered with generous transparent margin, no continents, no text.

### エラスティック・メッシュ
Transparent experimental warped mesh: intersecting thin white sine-distorted grid lines, fluid elastic topology, asymmetrical deformation, airy negative space, Y2K technical poster language, no fills, no text.

### トリプル・オービット
Transparent three-ring orbital diagram: three elegant elliptical rings on different axes, white and pale ice-blue with one acid-lime accent ring, small center target, minimal retro-futurist technical graphic.

### テクニカル・アロー
Transparent technical arrow: long white vector shaft, open triangular arrow head, measurement ticks in acid lime, circular registration marks near tail, precise engineering-label aesthetic, no text.

### スキュー・チェッカー
Transparent skewed checker ribbon: narrow black/white-free white checker band made only of white alternating blocks, perspective/skew feel, clean modular geometry, generous alpha padding.

### クローム・リキッドノット
Transparent isolated abstract liquid-chrome knot: asymmetric rounded metallic loop with one inner void, polished silver bands with subtle ice-blue and violet reflections, Y2K club-object aesthetic, no floor, no shadow plane, no text.

### ゼロックス・ハンド02
Transparent monochrome xerox collage hand: one reaching adult hand and wrist, five fingers, rough grayscale photocopy texture with halftone dots and ink grain, editorial cutout feel, generous alpha padding, no background or text.

### マイクロ・グレイン
Transparent 2048px micro-grain overlay: extremely fine mixed light/dark film grain, sparse xerox dust specks, very low alpha so underlying photos and typography remain readable, no focal object.

### リソ・カラーフィールド02
Full-bleed risograph color field: cobalt blue, vivid pink, acid yellow-green and violet soft overlapping airbrush fields, visible ordered print-dot texture, tactile but not dirty, no objects, no typography.

### ブルーバイオレット・ライトトレイル
Black-background screen overlay: long-exposure electric blue, violet, magenta and icy-white light ribbons crossing the lower half, optical bloom with ample dark negative space, suitable for Screen blending, no text.

### テクニカル・アーチ
Transparent photo frame: tall arch-shaped inner opening kept fully transparent, thin white technical outline, faint secondary oval construction line, acid-lime registration ticks, generous outer padding, no fill inside the photo area.
