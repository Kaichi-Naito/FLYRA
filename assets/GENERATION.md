# 素材の生成記録

内蔵 image_gen ツールで本アプリ向けに新規生成した6点です（CLI/API は不使用）。添付された作品そのものは同梱していません。生成PNGは加工せず、透明度を保ってコピーしています。

## grain-paper

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed raster overlay texture for a flyer design app, square 1024x1024. Create an original neutral medium-gray scanned print texture: extremely fine analog film grain, subtle xerox dust, a few faint scratches, matte risograph paper fibers. Uniform evenly distributed microtexture across the entire image, no focal point, no objects, no frame, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Average tone near 50% gray so it is useful with Overlay and Soft Light blending. Fine tactile print texture, NOT coarse stone or concrete.

## holo-gradient

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original full-bleed gradient background for editable music flyers, square 1024x1024. A liquid silver holographic iridescent gradient texture, cloudy pearl chrome with subtle lilac, ice blue and soft pink interference colors, one elegant flowing diagonal fold, luminous smooth soft diffusion, sophisticated early-2000s club poster aesthetic. No objects, no icons, no letters, no type, no borders, no logos, no watermark. Complete abstract texture suitable for overlays.

## heat-gradient

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original full-bleed raster gradient texture for a music flyer design app, square 1024x1024. Airbrushed cobalt blue melting into vivid vermilion and acid yellow in broad blurred circular fields, beautiful fine printed grain, analog thermal-map energy, smooth luminous organic color transitions, saturated but carefully composed with one off-center blue soft orb. Abstract only, no actual heat map labels, no objects, no text, no logos, no frame, no watermark.

## chrome-star

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated transparent PNG decorative sticker for a Y2K flyer editor. An original sculptural liquid chrome four-point star, elongated and delicately asymmetrical, crisp reflective silver with dark polished reflections and a very subtle ice-blue tint. Centered single icon occupying 80% of square canvas. Genuinely transparent background with alpha, no checkerboard baked in, no shadow plane, no text, no logos, no watermark, no additional symbols.

## orbital-globe

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated transparent PNG decorative sticker for a Y2K music flyer editor. One original futuristic orbital wireframe sphere tilted 20 degrees, crisp white latitude and longitude curves with one elegant thin elliptical orbit surrounding it. Flat white line artwork, no gray fills, no text, no continents, no logos, no watermark. Centered with generous clear margin on all edges, genuinely transparent alpha background, no black rectangle and no baked checkerboard. Square composition.

## ink-butterfly

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated transparent PNG sticker for original indie music flyers. A single graphic black ink butterfly silhouette with distressed white-cut internal wing veins and tiny star-like holes, symmetrical spread wings, bold high-contrast silkscreen graphic with slightly rough handmade edges. Centered, occupying 80 percent of square canvas, genuinely transparent alpha background. No letters or words, no logos, no watermark, no rectangle background, no drop shadow, no baked checkerboard. Original graphic, not a reproduction of an existing mark.
